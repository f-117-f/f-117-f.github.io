About

WHO WE ARE

X is a Canadian drone services company built for the demands of government and defence. We operate where precision matters and failure is not an option — from remote terrain surveys to live airspace interception.

We are not a generalist drone company. Every capability we offer is purpose-built for federal clients: DND, RCMP, CBSA, Public Safety Canada, and the defence contractors who support them.

Founded by a certified RPAS operator with hands-on field experience, X combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of Canadian government procurement — making us a reliable partner from first RFI to contract delivery.
